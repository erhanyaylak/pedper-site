02Aims & scope

What we publish

Contemporary scholarship in education — particularly teacher education, curriculum and instruction, learning sciences, social studies education and educational technologies. Submissions with strong methodological grounding, interdisciplinary relevance and international significance are particularly encouraged.

  • Teacher education and professional development
  • Curriculum design, instructional strategies and learning environments
  • Educational technologies, AI in education, digital literacy and digital competencies
  • Social studies, citizenship education and global competencies
  • Learning sciences; cognitive and affective learning processes
  • Assessment, measurement and educational evaluation
  • Inclusive, equitable and multicultural education
  • Interdisciplinary, comparative and international perspectives on education
Outside editorial scope
  • Manuscripts unrelated to education, pedagogy, teaching and learning, or educational policy and practice
  • Purely descriptive reports without a clear research problem, method and scholarly contribution
  • Non-scholarly opinion pieces without academic grounding or evidence
03Article types

Sections & word limits

All sections, including Vision Papers, go through double-blind peer review. Editorials are published by invitation for thematic issues. Abstracts are 250–300 words.

SectionScopeMain text
Research ArticlesOriginal empirical studies — qualitative, quantitative, mixed-methods, experimental, design-based, methodological6,000–10,000 words
Review ArticlesSystematic reviews, meta-analyses, scoping reviews, critical and integrative reviews7,000–12,000 words
Analysis / Case-BasedCase studies, practice-based investigations, policy analyses, context-specific qualitative studies5,000–8,000 words
Vision PapersForward-looking conceptual, theoretical or reflective contributions4,000–7,000 words
