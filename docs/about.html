About PedPer Publishing

A Türkiye-based, scholar-led academic publisher dedicated to education — committed to transparent, ethical and inclusive scholarly publishing.

01Who we are

Scholar-led publishing for a global readership

PedPer Publishing publishes Pedagogical Perspective (eISSN 2822-4841), an international, peer-reviewed journal in education founded in 2022. Our aim is to disseminate rigorous educational research to a global readership, free of charge for both readers and authors.

PedPer Publishing is operated by Scitera Information Technologies Ltd., based in the Giresun Technology Development Zone, Türkiye, and is part of the Scitera family of academic tools and services.

Our editorial and publishing practices are guided by the Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE), and every policy is publicly documented.

02What we stand for

Our principles

Diamond open access

All content is immediately and permanently free to read. No APCs, submission, review, page or colour charges.

Rigorous review

Double-blind peer review by at least two independent reviewers, guided by COPE’s ethical guidelines.

Editorial independence

A documented separation between publisher and Editor-in-Chief protects editorial decisions.

Non-commercial

Our journals accept no advertising and no commercial sponsorship that could influence editorial decisions.

03Governance

Separation of publisher and editor

To strengthen editorial integrity, accountability and independence, the Publisher and the Editor-in-Chief are different, independent people with distinct responsibilities.

  • No publisher influence. The publisher has no authority to influence, override or reverse editorial decisions.
  • Full editorial autonomy. The Editor-in-Chief decides independently on manuscript evaluation, peer review and publication.
  • Transparent conflicts of interest. Any potential conflict involving the publisher or the editor is managed under the journal’s COI policy.
  • Publicly documented. The structure is published openly for authors, reviewers and indexing bodies.

04Infrastructure

Technical infrastructure

Established, community-trusted services support every stage of publishing, discovery and preservation.

Open Journal Systems

Submission, peer review and publishing platform developed by the Public Knowledge Project (PKP).

Crossref DOIs

Every article receives a persistent DOI under the prefix 10.29329, with interoperable metadata.

LOCKSS · CLOCKSS · PKP PN

Distributed digital preservation networks secure long-term access to all published content.

OAI-PMH & feeds

Metadata is freely harvestable by libraries, aggregators and repositories; RSS and Atom feeds are available.

iThenticate

Plagiarism and similarity screening for every submission before peer review.

Paperpal by Editage

Complimentary language proofreading for all accepted manuscripts.
